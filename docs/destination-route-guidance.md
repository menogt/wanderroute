# Destination selection evidence

Reviewed 20 November 2026. These published examples inform optional planning ideas. Tour durations reflect each author's choices. They do not establish typical driving times, minimum stays, availability or what an independent traveller must book.

| Itinerary | Observation and design implication |
|---|---|
| [Visit My Sri Lanka](https://www.visitmysrilanka.lk/classic-sri-lanka-8-days/) | Eight days, CMB at both ends, two nights near Sigiriya and two near Galle. Group nearby sights before suggesting another hotel. |
| [G Adventures](https://www.gadventures.com/trips/journeys-discover-sri-lanka/8144/details/download/?language=en) | Visits Sigiriya from Dambulla and returns to the same base. A selected sight can be a day visit. |
| [Rough Guides](https://www.roughguides.com/sri-lanka/itineraries/7-days-in-sri-lanka-itinerary/) | A week strings together Colombo, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Ella and Hiriketiya. A useful journey can follow a corridor. |
| [Aman](https://www.aman.com/sites/default/files/2024-01/Aman_Sri_Lanka_Sample_itineraries_7N_Fort_and_Beach_Journey.pdf) | Seven nights split between Galle and the southern beach property. A two-base coastal trip can justify a return along the same road. |
| [One Sri Lanka Tours](https://www.onesrilanka.lk/guides/sri-lanka-7-day-itinerary) | Presents one-week alternatives by region, including a south-coast-only trip. Offer a choice when a short trip spans both coasts. |
| [Sri Lanka Tailor Made](https://www.srilankatailormade.com/itinerary/beyond-the-ordinary-sri-lanka/) | Ten days pair inland culture, Ella, parks and the southern shore. Night count matters more than the outline on a map. |
| [Encounters Travel](https://www.encounterstravel.com/tourinfo/sri-lanka-on-a-shoestring-tour) | A 13-day circuit starts and ends in Colombo, with an inland section. Distinguish a city endpoint from the international airport. |
| [RideWay Lanka](https://ridewaylanka.com/tours/14-day-sri-lanka-tour.html) | Fourteen days with multi-night stops and a private driver. Moving every night is optional. |
| [Sri Lanka Holidays Tours](https://www.srilankaholidaystours.com/packages/14-day-sri-lanka-tour-package-sigiriya-kandy-ella-yala-galle) | Two weeks link the hills and Ella to Yala, Galle and the western side. Compare the reverse order too. |
| [CeyRoutes](https://ceyroutes.com/itineraries/east-coast-sri-lanka) | A ten-day east-coast example pairs Sigiriya with Trincomalee and later coastal bases. A southern leg is optional. |
| [The Sri Lanka Guides](https://www.thesrilankaguides.com/itineraries/east-coast-sri-lanka-itinerary/) | A regional itinerary follows the eastern seaboard from Trincomalee towards Arugam Bay. A linear path can be intentional. |
| [In Between Travels](https://inbetweentravels.com/sri-lanka-a-2-week-itinerary/) | Two weeks include inland bases, Udawalawe and several south-coast days. Group beach time instead of accumulating overnight stops. |

The planner preserves every selection. The road comparison starts with the displayed order and includes the actual start and finish. It tries reversals and single-stop moves, using directional OSRM driving durations. The display thresholds (at least 30 minutes and 10% saved, or a last leg of at least 150 minutes) limit noise. They are product choices, not published travel rules. The comparison concerns car travel between the displayed stops only. It does not evaluate rail timetables, price, availability, activities or hotel commitments. Missing road data yields no time claim.
