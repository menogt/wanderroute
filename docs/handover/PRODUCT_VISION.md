# Complete traveler-first vision

Support the journey Discover -> Plan -> Customize -> Arrange bookings -> Prepare -> Arrive -> Travel -> Adapt -> Depart. An international visitor should describe a holiday, immediately receive a useful day-by-day itinerary, understand it geographically, edit important choices, see consequences, prepare outstanding arrangements and return to it during travel.

The result is the itinerary workspace, rather than a sequence of technical route-selection screens. Show duration/dates, party, ordered destinations and nights, interactive geography, daily experiences and descriptions, transfers, accommodation, budgeting, uncertainty, customization, preparation and current PDF. Alternatives are optional progressive disclosure within the workspace.

Classic is the minimum feature baseline: recognizable daily schedule, original map and road tracks, stays, transport, costs, export/share, saved trips and quote-request access. Preserve Classic while V2 improves these equivalents. Do not migrate unsupported Classic fixed prices, percentages or AI assertions into V2.

Preserve Explore and Classic's actual Leaflet tiles, branded pins, navy underlay, gold dotted route aesthetic, tooltips, popups and interactions. Road geometry must come from a routing service. Dotted straight city connections alone do not meet the owner's map requirement. A routing outage must leave editable itinerary/markers and a truthful message; no fabricated roads or times.

Customization must cover trip dates/duration, people, budget/currency, interests, pace, comfort, endpoints; destination additions/removal/order/nights and MUST/optional/excluded priorities; overnight bases versus day visits; multiple activities, search/add/remove/replace/order/move/timing/free time/restore; stays/areas/rooms/recorded rates; per-leg transport and arrangement status. Preview consequences. Preserve IDs, compatible edits, displaced shortlist and undo/redo. Protect locked dates/nights and independently booked stays/transfers.

Rationality takes priority over visual fullness: chronological flight dates, coherent night totals, late arrivals and recovery, departure windows, airport and return transfers, distinct meaningful attractions, geographical grouping and mode-appropriate estimates. Seven versus ten days must reconsider destination coverage; missing requested places stay explicit and missing MUST visits block preferred-draft finalization. Never fill a day with invented sights.

Useful preliminary cost estimation remains a product requirement. Ultimately supply evidence-based, date/source/currency/basis-qualified planning ranges for stays, intercity/airport/local transport, admissions/experiences, meals and miscellaneous costs; explain flights and other exclusions. Separate market estimates, provider quotes, traveler-recorded bookings and personal allowances. Unknown is not zero. Personal allowance entry alone does not satisfy the complete cost-estimation vision.

Review/finalization should establish a preferred proposed itinerary and preparation checklist, then eventually offer durable assistance/quote requests with honest receipt and notification status. A request is not a confirmed reservation. No unsupported booking confirmation, payment or incomplete V2 quote action may be enabled.

PDF: preserve the approved editorial navy/ivory/gold direction with geographical overview, complete day pages, stays, transfers, sourced/recorded costs, assumptions, warnings and preparation. Export the exact current revision including user edits; never a stale original. Return-to-plan and during-trip use require browser recovery and clear offline-copy guidance, without claiming full offline maps or cloud synchronization.
